Topic 02 · Curves and coordinates

A curve drawn by angle

In polar coordinates, a point is described by its distance from the origin and its angle. As the angle changes, a rule for the distance can trace out a whole curve.

The cardioid

A heart in one equation

Consider r = 1 + cos θ. For each angle θ, this rule gives a distance r. Plotting those points from 0 to 2π creates a cardioid.

r = 1 + cos θ

Here r is the distance from the origin, and θ is measured from the positive horizontal axis.

r = 1 + cos θ0 ≤ θ ≤ 2π

Reading the graph

How the shape appears

01

Farthest point

At θ = 0, cos θ = 1, so r = 2. The curve reaches its farthest point to the right.

02

The cusp

At θ = π, cos θ = −1, so r = 0. The curve comes to a point at the origin.

03

Symmetry

Cosine has the same value at θ and −θ, so the curve mirrors across the horizontal axis.
